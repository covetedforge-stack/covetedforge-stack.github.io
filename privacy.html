Privacy policy

inbox-triage is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Gmail account. It reads message headers and the beginning of message bodies in order to apply Gmail labels, archive messages, and manage the owner's filters and unsubscribes.

For messages not covered by a fixed rule, the sender, subject and up to 1,500 characters of the body are sent to the Anthropic API for classification. No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose. Google user data is handled in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
